Private foundation letter of inquiry template

A free, editable starting structure for small nonprofit teams. Follow the foundation’s own instructions first: confirm eligibility, whether it accepts inquiries, its requested format and length, and any required attachments. This template does not guarantee funding.

Replace the bracketed text with verified facts. Keep only the sections the funder requests. You can edit this template in your own document editor; no AI tool or upload is required.

[ORGANIZATION LETTERHEAD]
[DATE]
[PROGRAM OFFICER NAME OR APPROPRIATE RECIPIENT]
[FOUNDATION NAME]
[ADDRESS, IF REQUIRED]

Dear [RECIPIENT]:

On behalf of [ORGANIZATION], I am writing to request [AMOUNT] from [FOUNDATION] for [PROGRAM], which will [ONE-SENTENCE DESCRIPTION OF THE WORK].

Who we are and why this fits

[Briefly describe your organization’s mission, relevant experience and the connection between this work and the foundation’s stated priorities. Use facts you can verify.]

The need

[Describe the community or issue the program addresses. Support factual claims with reliable sources or verified organizational records; do not invent statistics or include identifiable beneficiary stories without appropriate permission.]

Our approach

[Explain the activities, who they serve, the timeframe and who will carry out the work.]

Anticipated outcomes

[State one or two measurable intended outcomes and how you plan to assess them. Distinguish goals from results already achieved.]

Funding request

[State the total project budget, the requested amount and what it would support. Identify other funding only as requested; distinguish confirmed support from applications or prospective sources. Check that every amount agrees with your budget.]

We would welcome the opportunity to discuss this request further. Thank you for considering [ORGANIZATION]’s work.

Sincerely,
[SIGNATORY NAME AND TITLE]
[CONTACT INFORMATION]

[ATTACHMENTS: list only those requested by the foundation.]

Before sending: remove placeholders; verify names, claims, figures and funder requirements; obtain your organization’s required approval. Keep donor, beneficiary and other confidential information out of public feedback or shared examples.
